# Yogamithra — The Smart AI-Based Yoga Mat 

> An AI-powered smart yoga mat concept that helps practitioners perfect their pose, track their practice, and get real-time feedback — like having a personal yoga instructor built into your mat.

**Status:** Concept / Early Planning Stage — no working prototype yet. This repository currently holds the project vision and will grow to include design docs, hardware plans, and code as development begins.

---

## The Idea

Most people practicing yoga at home have no way to know if their posture is correct. A wrong pose, held for a long time, can lead to strain or injury instead of benefit. **Yogamithra** aims to solve this by turning an ordinary yoga mat into a smart, AI-assisted companion that:

- Recognizes yoga poses (asanas) as the user performs them
- Detects incorrect alignment or posture in real time
- Gives instant corrective feedback (visual, audio, or haptic)
- Tracks sessions, streaks, and progress over time
- Guides beginners through routines without needing a live instructor

"Mithra" means *friend* — the goal is a mat that acts as a supportive companion to your practice, not just a surface to stand on.

##  Potential Tech Stack

Not finalized yet. Depending on the direction chosen, this project may involve:

- **Sensing:** pressure/flex sensors embedded in the mat, and/or camera-based computer vision
- **AI/ML:** pose estimation and classification (e.g. OpenCV, MediaPipe, TensorFlow/PyTorch)
- **Embedded/Hardware:** microcontroller (e.g. Arduino/ESP32) for sensor mats
- **App:** mobile or web app for feedback, tracking, and guided sessions

This section will be updated once the architecture is decided.
